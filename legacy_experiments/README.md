# 🧪 Legacy Experiments & Research Archive

This folder archives the original exploratory scripts, Jupyter notebooks, datasets, and benchmark results from the initial research phase.

## 📂 Contents

- **`notebooks/`**:
  - `loan_eligibility_ml.ipynb`: Original Jupyter notebook used for initial EDA, model experiments, and visualizations.
- **`scripts/`**:
  - `loan_eligibility_ml.py`: The original 600-line monolithic training script.
  - `compare_datasets.py`: Benchmark comparison script between original and synthetic datasets.
- **`models/`**:
  - Saved model `.pkl` checkpoints generated during early experimentation (`original_*.pkl`, `synthetic_*.pkl`).
- **`plots/`**:
  - Early visualization plots comparing models and log transformations.
- **`data/`**:
  - Archive copy of `loan_data.csv`.

---
> 💡 **Active Development**: All production code, the Web UI, REST API, modular pipelines, and current models are located in **`LoanTrack/`**.
